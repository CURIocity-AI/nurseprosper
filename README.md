# NurseProsper — Portfolio

Personal portfolio website for a Registered Nurse — showcasing experience, skills, specialties, and professional achievements.

## 🌐 Live Site

👉 **[https://CURIocity-AI.github.io/nurseprosper/](https://CURlocity-AI.github.io/nurseprosper/)**

## 📖 About

This is my personal nursing portfolio — a clean, modern website that presents my professional background, clinical skills, certifications, and contact information in one shareable link. Built for recruiters, hospitals, agencies, and professional connections.

## ✨ Sections

- 👩‍⚕️ **Hero / Intro** — Who I am and what I do
- 📖 **About Me** — My nursing journey, values, and approach to care
- 💼 **Experience** — Clinical roles, placements, and specialties
- 🎓 **Education & Certifications** — Degrees, licenses, and training
- 🩺 **Skills** — Clinical competencies and soft skills
- 📜 **Licenses & Credentials** — RN license, BLS, ACLS, etc.
- 📬 **Contact** — Email, LinkedIn, and direct message link
- 📄 **Download CV** — One-click resume download
